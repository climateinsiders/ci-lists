# Climate Insiders Investor Lists: 10 LinkedIn image prompts

For ChatGPT image generation (or Canva's generator). Paste the brand block plus one list block per image. The brand block is the same for all ten, so the ten posters read as one series. Numbers below come from the live Supabase tables on Oct 27, 2026; if a count changes, change it here too. Never invent a number for a poster.

If the generator mangles small text (it will, sometimes), tell it "keep every label exactly as written, prioritise text fidelity, fewer decorative elements". The headline, the four stats and the CTA matter most; the sticky-note tips can be dropped first.

Format: 4:5 portrait (1080 x 1350) for the LinkedIn feed.

## Canva test results (two renders, Oct 27)

Two posters were generated with Canva's image model from these prompts: `design/canva-test-us500.png` and `design/canva-test-eu500.png` (preview size). Verdict: the composition, the paper, the tape and the polaroids come out right, and the headline, four stats and CTA banner are legible. Small handwritten callouts and sticky-note tips get mangled about a third of the time. So: ChatGPT for the final ten (it holds small text better), Canva if you need a quick variant. Either way, check every number against the list block before posting.

Ink colour: the posters below use the dark green of the poster you sent. The landing page uses the All-Star kit (near-black ink #1F1B16, orange #FF7A1A). Both work together because the orange accent and cream paper are shared. If you want the posters to match the page exactly, replace "dark forest-green ink #0F3B2C" with "near-black ink #1F1B16" in the brand block.

## Brand block (paste first, every time)

Create a tall vertical poster (4:5) that looks like a hand-made scrapbook page on cream paper, in the style of a vintage travel journal. Follow this brand kit exactly.

Colours: warm cream paper #F4EFE4 with faint grain; dark forest-green ink #0F3B2C for all headlines, body text and rules; one accent, orange-red marker #E4652F, used only for the headline underline, the map dots and arrow tips; a butter-yellow highlighter #F3CF5A behind one headline word; pastel sticky notes in sage #CFE1CC, peach #F1C7BA, sky blue #BFD5E0 and butter yellow #F9E7A6; pastel washi tape on the polaroids. No pure black, no blue text, no gradients, no glossy effects.

Type: the big headline is a heavy condensed serif in dark green, all caps, two lines; the second line is rough brush-marker lettering sitting on a yellow highlighter stripe with an orange marker underline. Notes and callouts are casual handwriting (like Caveat). Labels, stats captions and the footer are small spaced capitals.

Structure, top to bottom:
1. Top edge: small spaced green capitals, left "CLIMATE INSIDERS", centre "INVESTOR LISTS", right the list region and "2026", thin rules between them.
2. Headline (two lines, exact text in the list block).
3. One handwritten subline (exact text in the list block).
4. Centre: a soft watercolor map of the region in muted sage and sand with orange-red dots on the cities named, three photo polaroids taped at angles with pastel washi tape, and four handwritten callouts with hand-drawn arrows (exact text in the list block).
5. A strip of four stats in heavy serif with small capitals captions (exact text in the list block).
6. Four pastel sticky-note cards, each with a tiny line icon and a handwritten tip (exact text in the list block).
7. Bottom: a dark green rounded banner with a speech-bubble icon and bold cream text COMMENT "KEYWORD" and, under it, small spaced capitals "AND WE'LL SEND YOU THE LINK".
8. Footer, tiny green capitals: "REAL PEOPLE. REAL CONTACTS. | SORTED BY REGION, THEN OFFICE. | BUILT FOR FOUNDERS AND FUND MANAGERS."

Flat print illustration, paper texture, tape, slightly imperfect handwriting, no photorealistic people, every word spelled exactly as written.
